Recommendations by what you want

  1. Easiest to learn: Ranger for a ranged playstyle, rated Easy-Medium and one of the more accessible damage classes for new players overgear timesaver.gg, or Gladiator if you want melee, pitched as one of the more forgiving melee classes to learn redwendy, KR. Avoid Assassin and Sorcerer as a first character, both rated Hard or harder overgear mmoexp.
  2. Safest for a first character: Ranger again, explicitly called "one of the safest overall picks with strong performance plus long-term role demand," and comfortable to solo-level without needing to tank hits ggwtb overgear.
  3. Best solo leveling: Spiritmaster, repeatedly called "one of the strongest solo classes" because its spirit acts as a built-in frontline tank aion2hub aion2boost. Gladiator and Cleric are close, more forgiving seconds u4n.com fextralife.
  4. Most wanted in dungeons: Cleric is confirmed near-mandatory as the only class that can actively dispel debuffs and carries a resurrection skill aion2hub fextralife. Templar is the tank counterpart, though that "near-mandatory" framing is contested and inferred rather than backed by a hard queue statistic contested, inferred aion2hub.
  5. Best PvP: Assassin is called top-tier PvP even after nerfs brought it "back to earth" ggwtb, contested against Ranger's own claim to A-tier as "the cleanest roaming pick after Assassin" contested ggwtb.
  6. Highest skill ceiling: Sorcerer, rated S-tier hardest to master mmoexp, and Assassin, explicitly named as having "the highest skill ceiling" of the roster timesaver.gg. No source ranks one clearly above the other.
Avoid taking any of this as settled: every tier and difficulty claim in this table is KR/TW-era, gathered before global launch and before any global balance pass. Several rest on boosting or carry-service tier lists marketing rather than independent testing, and even the more data-driven leaderboard source only reflects the KR/TW live meta, not global. Treat every ranking here as a snapshot that may already be stale by the October 5, 2026 global launch.
If you might want to swap classes later: none of the beginner-overview research for this guide documents a way to change an existing character's class, so switching later in practice means leveling a new character, an alt. Two facts from that research matter here. First, per-character systems including the Daeva Pass, Stigma builds, and gear do not share progress across characters, so trying a second class means rebuilding all of that from zero boostroom, 2026-02 mein-mmo, 2026-09. Second, once an account registers 84 combined dungeon clears within a single weekly lockout, tradeable Kinah and gear drops are reduced, one source says by 90 percent, for the rest of that week across every character on the account, not just the one that hit the threshold mmoexp, alt penalty youtube, kr-era. In practice, rolling one or two alts to try a different class looks fine; heavily dungeon-farming your main and a would-be replacement class in the same week is exactly the pattern that penalty targets.
