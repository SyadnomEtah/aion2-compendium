Progression roadmap

Tick milestones off as you hit them, your progress is saved in this browser, and you can export a backup from the toolbar at any time.

0

Before launch

Lock in the decisions that are hard or impossible to undo later. Pre-launch, now through October 4, 2026.

  • AION 2 is PC-only at global launch, playable through Steam or NCsoft's PURPLE launcher. No console or mobile version exists for the West yet.

  • Faction and server choice is effectively permanent, and Legions (guilds) are faction-locked, so this decides who you can play with.

  • Eight classes exist: Templar (tank), Gladiator and Assassin (melee DPS), Ranger (ranged DPS), Sorcerer and Spiritmaster (magic DPS), Cleric (healer), and Chanter (hybrid support).

  • This is open to anyone via Steam sign-up and is your first real chance to test performance and settings before launch.

  • Standard ($24.99) bundles 5 days of Advance Access plus 30 days of Membership, the same account-affecting items as Deluxe and Ultimate, which only add cosmetics.

  • Advance Access is a 5-day head start before the public October 5 global launch.

  • Recommended is Ryzen 7 3700X or i7-11700, 16GB RAM, RTX 2070, worth checking against your rig now rather than on launch day.

  • Membership is optional to play at all, but it gates trading on the Market and the Kinah to Quna currency exchange.

  • Moving Defiance off Spacebar, binding Sprint to Shift, and enabling combat overlays are a starting point to tweak in the Launch Scale Test, not gospel.

Done when: your faction, server, class, and Founder's Pack decisions are made, and you have logged in at least once during the Launch Scale Test or Advance Access.
1

First hours

Get moving without wasting anything you cannot get back. Level 1 to about 12, your first few hours.

  • Sealed Dungeons in Verteron and Altgard are solo, ungated by gear score, and pay out Daevanion Points from your very first hours.

  • Skills gain bonus chosen effects at level 8, 12, and 16, so a skill lagging behind these breakpoints will feel weak.

  • Flight is a core combat and traversal mechanic, not just cosmetic transport, and some Abyss boss mechanics reportedly require it.

  • Skill Points refund for a small Kinah fee, so trying a different core skill early carries little risk.

  • Spending limited daily keys before reaching max level reduces their endgame reward value.

  • This is when your first Daevanion board, Nezekan, becomes available, a good marker that you are through the opening hours.

Done when: you have cleared a Sealed Dungeon, unlocked your wings, leveled a core skill past 8, and reached level 12.
2

First talent board and first groups

Open the Daevanion board and start playing with other people. Level 12 to 23.

  • This is a permanent, separate-from-gear power source that guides call easy to overlook while rushing the story.

  • Node costs run grey 1, green 2, blue 3, orange 4 points. Tracing the cheapest path to nodes that support one loadout beats grabbing every nearby node for raw power.

  • This board is themed around damage amp and resist. The board level ladder is confirmed generally, but the exact stat theme is a single KR-era source and could shift for global.

  • Skills gain bonus chosen effects at level 8, 12, and 16, so a skill lagging behind these thresholds will feel clunky in group content.

  • It has a high drop rate for unique items and Kinah, and works in Explore Mode for alt characters too.

  • Solo players lose time to slow party-finding, bad pickup groups, and missed weekly group clears.

Done when: your Nezekan and Zikel boards are open, your core skills are past the level 12 breakpoint, and you are in a Legion.
3

Stigmas, energy, and your first real build

Commit to one PvE setup and start touching endgame-adjacent systems. Level 23 to about 40.

  • Stigmas are equippable skill-enhancement stones that add build-defining abilities on top of your class kit. They run on Stigma Shards, a separate currency from normal Skill Points.

  • Building a PvP set before your PvE rotation is stable wastes scarce Shards. A separate KR-era source describes a longer 23/30/40/45/50/55 slot ladder, so treat the total slot count as provisional until you see it in the client.

  • This board is themed around crit damage amp and resist, same board ladder as Nezekan and Zikel, with the stat theme sourced from a single KR-era article.

  • This party dungeon teaches aerial combat and group mechanics and drops pre-endgame gear ahead of the level cap.

  • A 5-player follow-up to Abyss Depths that drops more pre-endgame gear before you hit the level cap.

  • Arcana cards add item level, combat stats, set effects, and skill levels all at once, so an empty slot is wasted potential.

  • Weapon enhancement gives the single biggest felt damage boost. Enhancement materials are scarce once early one-time quests are used up, so save them for gear you will keep.

  • Only an officer can rent the hideout, for about 24 days at a time. Independent sources dispute that Legions have levels, a Legion Hall, warehouses, or buffs at all, so treat any "Legion level 5" claim as unconfirmed.

  • Pantheon is an easy-to-overlook permanent passive-stat system built from Statues and Colossi, separate from your Daevanion board.

Done when: you have a full 4-Stigma PvE set, have cleared Abyss Depths and Draconian Bastion once each, and your weapon leads the rest of your gear in enhancement.
4

Reaching cap

Close out the remaining boards and advanced Stigmas before settling in. About level 40 to 45.

  • Same board unlock pattern continuing from Nezekan, Zikel, and Vaizel, themed around multi-hit accuracy and resist.

  • KR/TW later raised this to 50, and global may launch with a separate "Level 20" skill and Stigma enhancement cap instead. NC has not clarified which numbers apply globally.

  • This closes out the confirmed 6-board Daevanion ladder. A single unverified source names two more boards, Marchutan and Yustiel, at the same level, so re-check this after launch.

  • Elyos run "The Shattered Stigma" and Asmodians run "The Shulak's Stigma" to unlock these slots.

  • Item level gates entry, but combat power, which also counts Daevanion, Pantheon, Arcana, and skill levels, decides whether you can actually clear the fight. Do not confuse the two.

  • These are the endgame source of Arcana cards and high-value drops once your gear can handle them.

Done when: all confirmed level-45 boards are open, you have your Greater Stigma slots, and you have cleared a Transcendence or Conquest dungeon.
5

First weeks at cap

Turn raw cap power into a repeatable weekly routine without wasting resources.

  • These give the best reward for the 40-energy reward cube cost of any content type.

  • Passive regeneration stops once the pool is full, so sitting capped wastes potential income.

  • One source cites 7 free entries a week, expandable via tickets, but exact weekly counts vary between sources, so verify in-client.

  • Check the achievement panel periodically for cheap stat titles rather than actively farming them. Titles come from PvP rank, Arena tiers, boss kills, and more.

  • The $15 a month Membership gates the player Market and the Kinah to Quna currency exchange.

  • Pushing enhancement further on gear you might replace soon wastes scarce Enhance Stones. +8 is described as a damage-smoothing milestone, especially for weapons.

  • Per-character systems like Battle Pass, Stigma builds, and gear do not share progress across characters, and an 84-run account-wide weekly soft cap discourages heavy alt farming for Kinah.

Done when: you have a repeatable weekly checklist you actually finish most weeks, and you have made a deliberate Membership and alt decision rather than drifting into one.
6

Re-verify after global launch

Check every KR-era assumption above against what actually ships on October 5, 2026.

  • NC has not clarified which cap number applies at global launch.

  • A separate KR-era source describes a longer 23/30/40/45/50/55 slot ladder, and a Stigma level cap raised from 20 to 25 past level 45. Neither is confirmed for global.

  • The source itself flags that this figure could change for global.

  • The confirmed board ladder is Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, Triniel, Ariel, and Azphel. A single unverified source adds these two more at level 45.

  • Sources disagree between a 560 cap with 15-per-3-hours regen and a weekly-accumulation reading closer to 1,400 total.

  • Expedition counts range from 3 to 21 per week and Transcendence from 2 to 14 per week depending on the source.

  • One source claims hundreds-of-players siege warfare is back, another says original-form sieges are unconfirmed. Separately, sources dispute whether Legions have levels, a Hall, or buffs at all.

  • Every reset-time figure found in research comes from KR/TW servers, not a confirmed global time zone.

  • The specific "90% reduction" figure comes from a single lower-trust source, though the existence of an 84-run soft cap is corroborated elsewhere.

  • Steam materials reportedly say 5-player, older teasers said 4-player, and one wiki cites 8-player expeditions.

Done when: you have checked each contested or KR-era item above against official patch notes or your own in-game UI after October 5, and updated your plan accordingly.
