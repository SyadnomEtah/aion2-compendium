Glossary Additions

More terms confirmed
Style Shop
The referenced but unnamed source of post-creation appearance changes; a special currency or cash-shop item is needed to alter your look after character creation finishes, exact item not yet named for global. Fextralife, date unknown
Rune (accessory)
An accessory-slot item distinct from Manastones, Theostones, and Arcana cards. Named examples: Clash Rune, Devotion Rune. Two good runes are worth roughly 80 item levels. Fextralife, date unknown kodex.yavuz, date unknown
Transfer Crafting
A single-source crafting process that carries a gear piece's enhancement, amplification, and Soul Binding progress into its next-tier upgrade, guaranteed to succeed, resulting item becomes Soul Bound. Unconfirmed by a second source. kodex.yavuz, date unknown
Legion Hideout
Rentable Legion space, only an officer can rent it, costing 1,000,000 Kinah for 23 days and 15 hours. Distinct from the disputed "Legion Hall" claim in the Legion card on the Systems page. guildmanager.app, date unknown
Abyss Rank (Trainee through Supreme Commander)
A 20-tier seasonal PvP ranking ladder built on Abyss Points. Ninth-Class Soldier needs 3,000 points; the single Supreme Commander slot needs 25,000,000. KR/TW server data. aion2hub, date unknown
Pet Genus Insight / Possession Effect
A mechanic where leveling and collecting pets grants passive Combat Power, separate from simply riding a pet as a mount. Vortex Gaming, date unknown
Odella
A high-tier gathering material (about 6 per gathering session) used to craft "Odella Energy," a Daevanion Crystal-equivalent material, plus higher-tier wings and equipment. Possibly the same underlying resource as this guide's "Odyle Energy," unconfirmed. Vortex Gaming, date unknown
Inder Boss
A boss encountered while gathering, defeating it is tied to Odella yield. Vortex Gaming, date unknown
