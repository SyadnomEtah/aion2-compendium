Money: what to buy, what to skip

AION 2 global is free to play, but a $15/month Membership gates the player economy, and Founder's Packs, a per-character battle pass, and a cosmetics shop all compete for your wallet. This panel ranks what is actually worth buying for a beginner.

Founder's Packs

On sale now, one per account confirmed

Buy Standard if you're playing at launch; skip Deluxe and Ultimate unless you want the skins.

Three tiers are on sale now on Steam and NC PURPLE. Only one purchase is allowed per account, and Steam does not let you upgrade to a higher tier later. PURPLE reportedly offers separate upgrade products. lagofast.com, date unknown

Is it worth it

Standard is repeatedly called the value pick because it includes the two gameplay-affecting items (head start, a month of Membership that normally costs about $15) at the cheapest price; the higher tiers only buy cosmetics. single source timesaver.gg, 2026

Some player discussion argues even Standard has situational value for name reservation and guild founding, even for players planning to go fully free to play. contested steam community, date unknown

Worth it: Standard Founder's Pack, if you plan to play at launch. It pays for the Membership you would buy anyway plus a head start.
Skip: Deluxe and Ultimate, unless you specifically want the cosmetic skins, pet, or wings. Zero functional gain over Standard.

Premium Membership benefits

$15 / month official confirmed

Membership is $15/month and gates the Market, Kinah/Quna exchange, and trading, not raw power.

NC's official July 8, 2026 announcement calls the subscription simply "Membership," priced at $15/month. massivelyop.com, 2026-07 aion2hub.com, date unknown

What you get

Confirmed benefits (official):

  • Unlocks trading on the Market (auction house) aion2hub.com, date unknown
  • Unlocks the Kina to Quna Exchange, converting earned gold into premium currency
  • Access to a special shop for extra purchases using Kina
  • Unspecified "additional gameplay benefits" NC has not itemized

Secondary sources claim extra storage and "enhanced progression benefits," but NC's own notice does not spell these out. single source u4n.com, date unknown

Not published: XP rate bonus, drop rate bonus, energy refill, auto-hunt duration, or exact storage slot counts. Do not assume parity with other MMOs' "premium buffs" until NC confirms.

Membership gates the auction house and currency conversion entirely for non-subscribers. Editorial coverage calls this the core of the monetization design: base gameplay is free, but real economic participation needs the subscription. mmorpg.com, 2026

Beginner tip: a casual player trying the game can skip it. Anyone planning to trade, gear up seriously, or play past the early levels will likely feel pressure to subscribe once trading and currency conversion matter. aion2hub.com, date unknown

KR/TW history

kr-era Korea/Taiwan originally launched a dual-subscription system instead: a Basic Membership (about $14 per 28 days, QoL framing) and an Expanded Membership (about $21 per 28 days, extra dungeon runs and higher caps), which drew heavy criticism. mmorpg.com, 2025 massivelyop.com, 2025-10 questlog.gg, date unknown

The global plan consolidated this into the single $15/month tier, reportedly after negative feedback. mmohuts.com, 2026

Situational: worth it once you want to trade, convert currency, or gear up through the market. Skip it if you're only trying the game casually.

Daeva Pass (battle pass)

Sold per character confirmed contested value

Wait on the Daeva Pass, it's sold per character and NC hasn't said if it affects power.

The battle pass is named the Daeva Pass. mmorpg.com, 2026 aion2hub.com, date unknown It is sold per character, not per account, which multiplies cost for players running alts. mmorpg.com, 2026

  • Two tracks: a free Standard Track for everyone, and a Premium Track purchased with Quna aion2hub.com, date unknown
  • Contents per NC's own description: "a combination of cosmetics, items, upgrade materials, and other in-game currencies." NC has not clarified whether "upgrade materials" affect power
  • Exact Premium Track price in USD or Quna was not found in any source checked

Because it is unclear whether "upgrade materials" grant a power edge, and per-character pricing punishes alt-friendly players, editorial coverage treats the Daeva Pass as the most ambiguous purchase in the whole stack, worth waiting on before buying. contested mmorpg.com, 2026

Skip for now: wait for NC to clarify whether Premium Track "upgrade materials" affect power before buying it blind, especially on more than one character.

Cash shop and currencies

confirmed

The shop sells cosmetics and consumables, not power, across four currencies: Kina, Quna, Mileage, Abyss Points.

Currencies

  • Kina (also spelled Kinah): free in-game gold from quests, kills, dungeons, events; used for gear, crafting, and the marketplace
  • Quna: premium currency, bought with real money or obtained by converting Kina through the Membership-gated Exchange
  • Mileage: a loyalty currency earned through play, used for select shop rewards
  • kr-era Abyss Points: PvP/Abyss currency, not purchasable with cash gamemeca.com, date unknown

Sources: aion2hub.com, date unknown aion2hub.com, date unknown

    Skip unless you want the specific skins.

P2W assessment and the KR controversy timeline

confirmed mechanism kr-era events

No direct power sales globally, but Membership indirectly lets paying players buy progression through the Market.

The mechanism

Nothing sold directly for cash is stated to increase power globally.

The pay-to-win pathway reviewers focus on is indirect: Membership gates the Market and the Kina-to-Quna Exchange, so a subscribed player can buy Kina-denominated gear from other players, buying progression speed without a direct "power item" purchase. contested, how large mmorpg.com, 2026 aion2hub.com, date unknown

That analysis argues Abyss (PvP) gear reportedly cannot be traded, insulating PvP somewhat, though spenders can still gear up faster indirectly via the Market. single source, opinion

KR/TW controversy timeline

  1. Nov 19, 2025
    KR/TW launch

    40 servers go live; the cash shop sells items that directly boost combat power (Combat Enhancement Scrolls, Soul Book/Soul Binding items) using Quna, despite prior promises such items would not be sold massivelyop.com, 2025-11.

  2. Within days
    NCSoft stock drops

    Stock reportedly fell more than 15% within a couple of days of launch massivelyop.com, 2025-11.

  3. ~15 hours post-launch
    Emergency broadcast and apology

    Producer Kim Nam-joon held an emergency broadcast, admitted the team had been "complacent and unthoughtful," and apologized publicly massivelyop.com, 2025-11.

  4. Remediation
    NC pulls the bundles

    NC removed the offending cash-shop bundles, gave the gear-enhancing items to all players for free, removed skill-reset costs, and halved cash-shop consumable prices.

  5. First 6 weeks
    ~$70 million earned regardless

    Despite backlash, AION 2 reportedly earned about $70 million across Korea and Taiwan, peaking around 2.5 billion KRW (about $1.6 million) daily single source, approximate boosting-ground.com, date unknown.

  6. Since the controversy
    Cosmetics-only stance

    NCSoft producers have publicly stated the shop will sell cosmetics only going forward, "leaning toward skins, mounts, and pets" massivelyop.com, 2025-11.

  7. Jul 8, 2026
    Global plan formalizes this

    NC's official global monetization notice states cosmetics have "no gameplay or combat advantage" and consumables "do not affect gear progression or long-term power." aion2hub.com, date unknown

    It also consolidates the dual subscription into one $15/month tier. mmohuts.com, 2026

Not confirmed: no source has NC stating, in its own words, that the specific KR-era combat-boosting items are permanently banned from the West. Treat "it definitely won't happen again globally" as consistent with NC's current stated plan, not as a guarantee.

The overall size of the pay-to-win gap cannot be quantified precisely. The mechanism (subscription gating trade access, plus a cash-to-Kina exchange) is confirmed by NC's own plan; how much faster a paying player can gear up is analyst opinion, not an NC-published number. contested

Region, pricing, and refund notes

mostly single source

Prices are USD-only so far; refunds follow Steam's usual window, and account linking is permanent.

  • Prices quoted throughout are USD as listed in Western coverage; no separate EUR pricing was found published.
  • kr-era KR pricing was denominated in KRW (19,700 to 29,800 KRW per 28-day tier) and does not directly translate to the global USD figures.
  • Refund policy: cited as the general Steam-style window (14 days, under 2 hours played, no bundled Founder's Pack items claimed), but this is a general guide statement, not a quoted official AION 2 refund policy. single source, verify with Steam
  • Steam funds hold: one source claims Steam-spent funds are unusable/untransferable for 60 days after purchase; unverified, confirm with NC or Steam support before relying on it. single source, unverified
  • Account linking: linking a Steam account to an NC account is reported as permanent and irreversible; decide which account you intend to use long-term first lagofast.com, date unknown.
  • Founder's Pack upgrades: contents are reported identical regardless of platform, but tier-upgrade purchases are reportedly only available via PURPLE, not Steam, per the same source, not independently corroborated lagofast.com, date unknown.
