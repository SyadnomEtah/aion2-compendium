Character Progression Systems

AION 2 layers well over a dozen progression systems on top of your base class. The cards below are listed roughly in unlock order, from the skills you touch at level 1 through to endgame Combat Power grinding, so you can read top to bottom as a rough roadmap.

Global launches October 5, 2026, and most numbers here come from the KR/TW live client, so treat exact thresholds as subject to change.

Mastery Skills and Specialization

Available from lvl 1, breakpoints at 8, 12, 16 confirmed marketing

Mastery Skills level automatically; spend Skill Points on your core 3-4 rotation abilities.

What it is

Mastery Skills are your core class abilities. They are learned automatically as you hit level requirements. expcarry, date unknown

Every level-up grants Skill Points, which you spend to raise a Mastery Skill up to a base cap of 10. YouTube, date unknown LDCloud, date unknown

How it works

  • Skill Points can be refunded for a small Kinah fee, so early experimentation is safe Fextralife, date unknown.
  • Daevanion board and gear effects can push a skill's effective level to 12 or 16, past the base cap of 10 Fextralife, date unknown.
  • At skill breakpoints of level 8, 12, and 16, you can pick up to 3 additional chosen effects, called Specialization Fextralife, date unknown oslink, date unknown.
  • Extra Skill Points come from Empyrean Traces, world collectibles turned in at a Monolith for Wisdom Stones, an item that grants Skill Points on use. Fextralife, date unknown

    A separate guide calls the same collectible simply "Feathers" turned in at city Monoliths. Differ, date unknown

Korean class-board guides from 2026 routinely talk about pushing core skills to skill level 20, and some to 25, above the 8/12/16 breakpoints described here. That ceiling is reached with Daevanion, Stigma and Arcana bonuses stacked on the base cap, and no single source states the current maximum, so treat the top breakpoint as KR-era and unverified for global kr-era inven, 2026-02 dcinside, 2026. The per-class Leveling build cards give the skill-level targets those guides use.
Naming is inconsistent between sources: "Empyrean Traces"/"Wisdom Stones" versus "Feathers" are likely different translations of the same KR-era item, so treat the exact item names as contested.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: put points into your main damage chain, a self-heal or HP recovery skill, and anything usable while moving, then push your rotation's core 3-4 abilities past the 8/12/16 breakpoints expcarry, date unknown.

Common mistake

Common mistake: spreading Skill Points thin across many skills instead of concentrating on your rotation's core abilities.

Stigma System

First slot unlocks lvl 23 contested marketing

Stigmas add build-defining abilities; sources disagree on exactly which levels unlock which slots.

What it is

Stigmas are equippable skill-enhancement stones that add build-defining abilities on top of your class kit. expcarry, date unknown They consume a separate currency, Stigma Shards, rather than normal Skill Points. expcarry, date unknown

Unlock and cost

  • One detailed source says the first Stigma slot unlocks at level 23, with two more normal slots at 30 and 40, two Greater Stigma slots at 45 and 50, and a Major Stigma slot at 55 Aion Nova, date unknown Differ, date unknown.
  • A second source instead says Stigmas unlock at level 23 with up to four equipped at once, which conflicts with the 23/30/40/45/50/55 breakdown above aion2hub, date unknown.
  • Level 45 is confirmed to unlock two Greater Stigma slots via a group quest, "The Shattered Stigma" for Elyos or "The Shulak's Stigma" for Asmodians Differ, date unknown.
  • KR-era content raised the Stigma level cap from 20 to 25, requiring a higher-tier Shard above level 20; this is explicitly flagged as KR-only and unconfirmed for global expcarry, date unknown kr-era.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: pick a single PvE rotation and fund the Stigmas that fill actual gaps in it, upgrading your most-used Stigma first rather than spreading Shards across every Stigma you own expcarry, date unknown Overgear, date unknown.

Common mistake

Common mistake: building a PvP Stigma set before you have a stable PvE rotation, which wastes scarce Shards.

Daevanion Board

First board unlocks lvl 12 confirmed

Six Daevanion Boards unlock through level 45 and add permanent combat stats separate from gear.

What it is

A permanent node-board progression system, separate from gear, that raises combat stats and effective skill levels. Overgear, date unknown aion2hub, date unknown

Unlock and cost

  • The first board unlocks at level 12; one detailed source states there are 6 total boards unlocking progressively, with the last two arriving at level 45 Fextralife, date unknown Talentbuilds, date unknown.
  • Daevanion Points unlock nodes; Points and Daevanion Crystals both come from regional quests, Sealed Dungeons, Strongholds, achievements, and exploration Overgear, date unknown expcarry, date unknown Differ, date unknown.
  • The KR client can reset a whole board or individual nodes, with a single-node reset costing 500 Kinah in Korea. expcarry, date unknown kr-era, self-flagged as provisional

    A node cannot be removed while another already-acquired node depends on it. The source explicitly flags that Global's final reset costs, caps, and terminology may differ from what shipped in Korea.

    Separately, Korean community discussion claims a recent patch "significantly reduced" reset costs for both skills and Daevanion nodes, without giving a before/after number, consistent with but not independent confirmation of the 500 Kinah figure. single source, vague liveissue.kr, date unknown

How it works

  • Gold/orange nodes are passive stat boosts, blue nodes amplify active skills, and green nodes optimize existing passives like resource regen, per one summary. expcarry, date unknown

    A second source describes essentially the same split as "skill nodes" and "stat nodes" without the color breakdown, a likely simplification rather than a contradiction.

  • The board is cited as a source of 500+ "passive" Combat Power that does not require gear swapping, earned by clearing hidden question-mark dungeons, Sealed Dungeons, and Strongholds u4n, date unknown.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: trace the cheapest path to skill/stat nodes that support one specific loadout, comparing full routes by total point cost rather than grabbing every nearby node purely for Combat Power expcarry, date unknown.

Common mistake

Common mistake: neglecting the board while rushing the main story; it's a significant permanent power source that's easy to overlook Overgear, date unknown.

Arcana

Obtained mainly from Transcendence dungeons single source marketing

Arcana cards add item level, stats, and set bonuses; fill every slot rather than chasing perfect rarity.

What it is

A card-based system that adds Item Level, combat stats, set bonuses, and effective skill levels all at once. expcarry, date unknown

  • Original slot types are Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, and Mirror; later content reportedly added Scales, Key, Hourglass, Dice, and Lantern, for up to nine slots total. expcarry, date unknown Vortex Gaming, date unknown

    More precisely, the card-slot rollout came in generations: the original five (Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, Mirror), Scales added in "Season 2," and Key, Hourglass, Dice, and Lantern added in "Chapter 1." single source expcarry, date unknown

  • Rarity tiers cited are Common (about +20 item level), Rare (+40), Epic (+60), and Unique (+80), affecting a card's overall item-level contribution. expcarry, date unknown

    A separate source uses "green 40, blue 60, unique 80" for the same slots, suggesting a rarity-name translation mismatch between sources.

How it works

  • Cards come primarily from Transcendence dungeons tied to specific slots, sets, and crystal families, expcarry, date unknown Overgear, date unknown plus crafting/synthesis, extraction from unwanted cards, and events.

    The guidance is to pick a dungeon for its Arcana output rather than for its difficulty stage alone. single source expcarry, date unknown

  • Cards gain experience from Training Arcana consumables (Lesser 200 EXP, Intermediate 600, Superior 1,500, Ultimate 3,000) or by feeding in other cards. expcarry, date unknown

    Skill-level bonuses stack with Stigma and Daevanion bonuses toward the same 16-20 breakpoints. Equipping five different cards is separately said to "significantly increase" skill levels and Combat Power. aion2hub, date unknown

  • Targeted cards can also be crafted directly using tiered crystals, Mysterious, Harmonious, Noble, or Splendent Noble depending on card generation, plus Kinah single source, detailed expcarry, date unknown.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: fill every slot with something usable rather than waiting for a perfect Unique card, protect skill breakpoints and set bonuses over raw item level, save Training Arcana for cards you plan to keep, and farm the Transcendence dungeon that matches the slot or crystal family you actually need expcarry, date unknown.

Common mistake

Common mistake: leaving slots empty, farming the wrong dungeon for prestige, over-enhancing filler cards, or destroying a card before confirming its replacement is actually equipped expcarry, date unknown.
Flag: this progression system, dungeon-gated drops, tiered EXP materials, and crystal crafting, is exactly the kind of thing that could change materially for global launch, so treat every figure above as KR-era kr-era. No official global skill-name glossary exists either, so card-to-skill assignments quoted elsewhere on this page are fan translations, not confirmed localizations mmoculture, 2026-07.

Equipment Grades and Enhancement

Gear from questing, loot, and the Broker contested marketing

Gear grade names vary by source; enhance evenly across slots before pushing any one piece high.

What it is

NCSoft's own patch notes reportedly confirm at least Heroic and Unique grades, with Unique higher. exitlag, date unknown

Community sources also describe a wider tier list, Rare from standard dungeons, Unique from Hard Mode, Legendary from Nightmare/Abyss content, alongside a separate Green/Blue/Purple/Gold color scheme used for Pantheon items, so grade naming is not fully consistent across sources.

How it works

  • Enhance Stones raise a gear piece's level; enchanting adds PvE Attack/Defense on PvE gear or PvP Attack/Defense on PvP gear depending on item type exitlag, date unknown.
  • Failing an enchant does not destroy the item, and extracting a previously-enhanced item refunds 100% of the Enhance Stones spent into it exitlag, date unknown Fextralife, date unknown.
  • Suggested safe breakpoints to stop and bank Kinah: +3-5, +8 ("damage smoothing", especially for weapons), +10 (feels properly progressed), +12 (controlled risk on good gear), and +15 (only for long-term keeper gear) BoostRoom, date unknown.
  • Mana Stones socket into gear for stats like Crit Rate or Speed, helping gear "scale into late-game without major farming" alongside Soul Codexes. u4n, date unknown Fextralife, date unknown

    A separate single source describes an "engraving" mechanic where one attempt rerolls every socket, uncorroborated elsewhere.

  • Gear comes from questing, world/dungeon loot, and the player marketplace or Broker Fextralife, date unknown.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: enhance your weapon first since it gives the single biggest damage boost, then survivability armor, then remaining armor, then accessories last BoostRoom, date unknown Overgear, date unknown.

Common mistake

Avoid: pushing one piece to a high, risky enhancement level before your other slots have reached a low safe tier.

Manastones and Theostones

Socketed into gear and weapons confirmed

Mana Stones and Theostones are socketed gems and weapon procs, slot them in as they drop.

What it is

Mana Stones are AION 2's socketed stat gems, found via dungeons, rewards, or crafting. u4n, date unknown Fextralife, date unknown Theostones are the weapon proc-stone equivalent of classic AION's Godstones.

How it works

  • Theostones socket into a weapon's dedicated slot and grant a chance-on-hit proc effect such as damage or a debuff; one effect per weapon, and identical Theostones do not stack aion2.online, date unknown.
  • A community database lists 153 known Theostones, corroborated across three independent sources aion2.online, date unknown aion2hub, date unknown.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: treat Mana Stones and Theostones as gradual scaling tools you slot in as they drop, rather than something to farm dedicated runs for early on.

Soul Binding

Grows once bound, per one source single source

One uncorroborated source says bound gear gains a growing secondary stat; bind and use gear normally.

What it is

Described by one fan-run compiled wiki as a secondary stat block on gear that "only grows once you bind it." kodex.yavuz, date unknown This is likely analogous to classic AION's soul-binding-on-equip mechanic but with an added stat-growth twist here.

Not corroborated by any other source found; treat the whole mechanic as single-source and uncertain until confirmed elsewhere.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: bind and use gear normally, since even the single source describing this doesn't suggest a reason to delay binding.

Pantheon

Domains cap at 200 points each single source cluster marketing

Pantheon is an unconfirmed passive-stat system, slot pieces in opportunistically, don't farm it dedicated.

What it is

An "often overlooked" permanent passive-stat system built from Statues, Colossi, and "Artwork"/frameworks; placing a Colossus into a Pantheon slot grants points toward one of several "deity domains," each capping at 200 points before converting into flat combat stats. Vortex Gaming, date unknown rsnxt, date unknown

How it works

  • Item rarity (Green to Blue to Purple to Gold) determines each piece's bonus strength, with Green giving 40 stat points and Blue 60 in one cited example.
  • Named domains include Wisdom (strike chance/final damage), Freedom (accuracy/evasion), and Death (critical hit chance) Vortex Gaming, date unknown.
This system rests on a small cluster of corroborating community sources, mostly one aggregator plus a boosting site, with no wiki or database confirmation found. Treat the whole Pantheon system as not independently confirmed, though the name itself appears consistently.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: treat Pantheon pieces as passive value you slot in opportunistically rather than a system worth dedicated farming until it's better confirmed for global.

Titles, Achievements, and Collections

Title slots reported around lvl 30 contested

Achievements and titles come from normal play, don't farm them as a separate track.

What it is

Achievements grant Achievement Points and rewards, and completing them ties into Daevanion Point/Crystal income. Differ, date unknown

How it works

  • One database lists 1,307 achievements, while a differing count elsewhere cites 4,225 named achievements across grades, categories, and factions, of which 2,685 grant rewards; likely different snapshots or counting methods aion2hub, date unknown.
  • Titles: one source counts 1,472 total titles with 844 granting stat bonuses, earned via PvP rank, Arena tiers, boss kills, monster-hunting milestones, server-first accomplishments, and mini-game/event wins. aion2hub, date unknown

    Stat titles grant things like PvP Attack, PvP Accuracy, HP, Defense, Combat/Move Speed, or Critical Hit; some titles are purely cosmetic.

  • A separate source states you can have 3 active title effects at once across Offensive/Defensive/Other categories, unlocking around level 30; this slot count and level could not be cross-confirmed, treat as single-source.
  • An Appearance Collection (wardrobe) exists; collecting roughly 700 appearances grants combat speed and cooldown reduction as a milestone bonus. Vortex Gaming, date unknown

    Cash-shop cosmetics do not count toward this bonus, though they do count for achievements.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: don't farm titles or achievements as a separate track early; treat them as a byproduct of normal questing and dungeon clears, and periodically check the achievement panel for cheap stat titles you already qualify for.

Pets and Mounts

200+ pets collectible confirmed

Pets double as mounts, farm 10 "souls" of a monster to unlock each one.

What it is

Pets and mounts are the same system in AION 2: any pet you own can also be ridden as a mount. u4n, date unknown Fextralife, date unknown Over 200 pets are collectible. BoostRoom, date unknown

How it works

  • The main method is farming 10 "souls" of almost any monster to unlock it as a pet/mount; souls also drop from the Shugo Shop and the Black Cloud Merchant, and rare mounts can come from Hidden Cubes Fextralife, date unknown.
  • The in-game Pets menu has a "Source" tab showing exactly where a given soul drops.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: don't ignore world mobs while leveling, many double as free future mounts once you've collected 10 souls; check the Source tab before grinding blindly for a specific pet.

Wings and Flight

Earned around lvl 10 (unverified) confirmed

Flight is a real combat mechanic, not cosmetic; wings and mounts are complementary tools.

What it is

Flight is a core combat and traversal mechanic, not just cosmetic transportation; some Abyss boss mechanics reportedly require flight to execute correctly. TheGamer, date unknown u4n, date unknown

Unlock and cost

  • Wings are not available from level 1; one single, unverified source gives a rough unlock around level 10.

How it works

  • Wings have a "flight power" stat acting as airborne stamina; upgrading a wing raises max flight power via a physics-based "Glide Gauge" rather than a rigid timer TheGamer, date unknown u4n, date unknown.
  • Only wings earned through gameplay grant flight power; purchased cosmetic wing skins sit in a separate skin slot layered over your earned wings and provide no flight benefit TheGamer, date unknown.
  • Higher-quality wings also add flat Attack/Defense/HP and can grant movement speed or special combat effects.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: use mounts as your short-range sprint tool and wings/gliding as your long-range terrain-skip tool, they're complementary, not redundant. Manage your Glide Gauge deliberately, since running out mid-air just drops you.

Legion

Up to 128 members contested marketing

Legions cap at 128 members and offer organization and group access; the level, hall and buff perks below are disputed.

What it is

AION 2's guild system, capped at up to 128 members per Legion, used for PvE, PvP, crafting, and social access. exitlag, date unknown 6Sword, date unknown Legion perks are mostly about organization and access to reliable groups rather than direct stat buffs. mmoexp, date unknown

How it works

  • Legion Hall unlocks at Legion level 5, costs 1,000,000 Kinah to rent, and houses cooking, alchemy, jewelcrafting, armorsmithing, and blacksmithing merchants/workbenches together, the real convenience benefit mmoexp, date unknown.
  • Legion Coin is earned for active contribution and spent on Legion-exclusive skins, uniforms, masks, mounts, and wings at specialized merchants u4n, date unknown.
  • Legion levels beyond 5 raise the member cap and unlock member access to purchase or use new Abyss weapons and armor mmoexp, date unknown.
Disputed: the Legion level, Legion Hall unlock and Abyss-gear perks above come from a single boosting-adjacent source. Three independent sources say Legions have no levels, warehouse or mechanical buffs, and a developer statement from February 2026 agrees. See the "Legion: Rank Structure and the Warehouse/Buff Controversy" card further down this page.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: join an active Legion early for group content access and Legion Hall crafting convenience; don't expect membership itself to be a large stat boost.

Companions / Summoned Spirits

Class kit feature, not universal single source

There's no universal companion system, only the Spiritmaster's four elemental summon spirits.

What it is

There is no evidence of a general, class-agnostic "companion" or "summon" progression system in AION 2.

The only summon-type mechanic found is class-specific to the Spiritmaster, which channels four elemental spirits (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth) as combat pets/turrets with independent attack capability, used to chain skill activations for DPS. Fextralife, date unknown mmoexp, date unknown

Treat "Companions" as not a universal system; it is a Spiritmaster kit feature, not something every character has.

Stats That Matter

PvE and PvP stats are fully separate single source marketing

PvE and PvP stats are fully separate pools; build your PvE set first and ignore PvP caps for now.

What it is

Accuracy governs the chance to land an attack and, per one source, also affects crit chance; Precision is a hybrid stat raising both Accuracy and Crit chance. u4n, date unknown

How it works

  • PvE Attack/Defense only comes from PvE gear and only affects damage to monsters; PvP Attack/Defense only comes from PvP gear and only affects damage to other players exitlag, date unknown.
  • One source states endgame PvE targets are roughly Accuracy 2350 and Critical Hit 2600-2700 depending on synergy/buffs. u4n, date unknown

    The Combat Power formula itself stops rewarding further Accuracy above 1700 or Crit above 80%, making stacking past those levels inefficient for Combat Power purposes.

  • Because burst damage is high (one source claims an Assassin can deal about 80% of a target's HP in one combo), the recommended early PvP stat order is HP, crit resistance, mitigation, then PvP Defense. BoostRoom, date unknown

    PvP Attack is added only once survival is established.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: build one PvE stat set first and don't chase PvP stat caps until you're actually PvPing regularly.

Combat Power ("Atul") and Item Level

Item Level gates content; CP is an aggregate score single source marketing

Item Level gates content access; Combat Power is a separate aggregate score, raise both from cheap broad sources first.

What it is

There appear to be two related but distinct numbers: Item Level, which gates access to activities such as Transcendence content around 900-2700+ tiers, and Combat Power or "Atul Score," an aggregate power estimate used for matchmaking/ranking-style comparisons. Fextralife, date unknown u4n, date unknown

The "Atul" naming is single-source and could be a translation artifact; treat with caution.

How it works

  • Fastest ways to raise Combat Power, per multiple sources:
    • Even gear enhancement across all slots up to a low safe tier before pushing any one piece further
    • Filling Daevanion board nodes (500+ passive CP cited)
    • Collecting/upgrading Arcana cards (400+ CP cited)
    • Raising Accuracy toward roughly 1200+ via Ariel, wings, magic stones, and the Sanctuary Bracelet
    • Completing early permanent items like the Noble Belt and Revelation Amulet Vortex Gaming, date unknown u4n, date unknown
  • Rushing the Yellow (Main Story) quest line is also cited as a fast way to raise base stats and thus CP u4n, date unknown.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: chase the cheap, broad CP sources first (Daevanion nodes, Arcana, main story) before grinding for marginal gear enhancement gains.

Transcendence and Odyle Energy

Entry tickets removed as of Jul 2026 patch confirmed kr-era

Odyle Energy opens reward cubes after a clear, not dungeon entry, and it's shared across normal and Transcendence runs.

What it is

Transcendence is AION 2's endgame progression/ranking layer, class-ranked, fed by specific dungeons such as "Phantom Corridor" with up to 4 escalating difficulty levels that raise the minimum entry Combat Power and monster strength; main rewards are Arcana cards plus Breakthrough Stone Fragments and Stigma Shard Fragments (single source, KR-era).

How it works

  • As of a July 1, 2026 patch, entry tickets for Expedition and Transcendence dungeons were removed; instead, Odyle Energy is spent only to open the reward cube after clearing a run. aion2hub, 2026-07 u4n, date unknown

    A normal boss cube costs 40 energy and Hard difficulty costs 60, and normal/Transcendence dungeons draw from the same shared energy pool.

  • One source states Transcendence content becomes available around gear score ~1650, unverified elsewhere kodex.yavuz, date unknown.
  • No evidence was found of a traditional "class advancement" or ascension tier in AION 2.

    What exists instead is Transcendence dungeons plus "Awakening Battles," timed solo encounters with extra objectives and temporary combat bonuses, a separate activity track from Episode/Region/Mission content (single source, could not verify further).

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: entry itself isn't gated by energy, so you can scout Transcendence dungeons early, but budget your Odyle Energy between normal and Transcendence content since they share one pool.

Crafting, Gathering, and Cooking

Five professions, gathering feeds all of them confirmed marketing

Five crafting professions share one gathering skill; pick one or two rather than spreading thin.

What it is

Five primary crafting professions are cited: Blacksmithing, Armor Smithing, Handicrafting, Alchemy, and Cooking. exitlag, date unknown expcarry, date unknown Cooking produces food/drink buffs used before dungeons and PvP.

How it works

  • Gathering is done via an "Essence Extraction" skill/action used to mine ore, gather herbs, or cut wood, feeding all five crafting lines.
  • No evidence found of a dedicated dyeing system; classic AION had player-dye mechanics, so its absence here is flagged as unverified rather than confirmed absent.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: pick one or two gathering skills that match your intended crafting profession rather than spreading thin, and use the Legion Hall once available (Legion level 5) to access all five crafting stations in one place mmoexp, date unknown.
