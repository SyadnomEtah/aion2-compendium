Beginner mistakes to avoid

Compiled and de-duplicated from three guides covering launch mistakes and progression traps. Numbers like item-level thresholds are KR-era and may shift for global.

Account and social setup mistakes

confirmed

Faction/server choice is permanent, and skipping a Legion costs you time and mentor perks.

  • Picking a faction or server casually. It is effectively permanent and determines who you can play with. confirmed, 2+ sources mein-mmo.de, 2026-09 boostroom.com, 2026-02
  • Not joining a Legion (guild). Solo players lose time to slow party-finding and missed weekly group clears.

    It also means more bad pickup groups. single source boostroom.com, 2026-02

    Avoid: staying solo through choice rather than circumstance, group content and the mentor system both reward being in a Legion.

Gear and materials mistakes

confirmed

Save upgrade materials, skip the pricier Abyss scrolls, and don't over-invest in gear you'll replace.

  • Selling or discarding gear-upgrade materials (enhancement fragments) instead of saving them. They are scarce once early one-time quests are used up confirmed, 2+ sources mein-mmo.de, 2026-09 boostroom.com, 2026-02.
  • Ignoring cheaper "grey" Abyss scroll options at weekly vendors that give identical rewards to pricier variants single source boostroom.com, 2026-02.
  • Prioritizing low-value daily dungeon rewards (pets, Kinah) over enhancement stones needed for gear progression single source boostroom.com, 2026-02.
    Avoid: grabbing the shiny reward over the one that actually feeds your gear pipeline.
  • Enhancing gear you will replace soon, wasting scarce upgrade materials on temporary items confirmed, 2+ sources mein-mmo.de, 2026-09 boostroom.com, 2026-02.

Systems and scheduling mistakes

confirmed

Don't spend keys early, skip valuable daily quests, or attempt weekly content before you're ready.

  • Spending limited daily keys (for example, Shugo Fiesta) before reaching max level, which reduces their endgame value. single source boostroom.com, 2026-02
  • Picking the wrong daily quests and missing ones that reward valuable bound keys or fragments. single source boostroom.com, 2026-02
  • Attempting the Ascension Trial or other high-end weekly content before meeting its real item-level requirement, wasting a limited weekly attempt.

    single source boostroom.com, 2026-02

    Avoid: guessing your readiness, check item level and combat power against the requirement first.
  • Avoiding the Abyss/PvP entirely. Non-PvP players can still earn Abyss points through supply quests and PvE kills, which feed gear and Stigma systems single source boostroom.com, 2026-02.
  • Playing with no weekly plan at all. Repeatedly called out as the single biggest efficiency mistake, since weekly caps and high-value activities matter more than hours logged confirmed, 2+ sources mein-mmo.de, 2026-09 boostroom.com, 2026-02.
  • Misusing macros in ways that risk an account flag, stick to the sanctioned two-skill auto-attack macro rather than third-party automation single source.
  • Over-grinding materials using outdated KR-original farming routes once global numbers differ single source.

Combat and build mistakes

confirmed

Don't grind blind: mix in side content, commit to one build, and confirm skill slots and item level vs combat power.

  • Leveling purely through grinding and XP while ignoring side content, arriving at cap under-geared and under-built confirmed, 2+ sources mein-mmo.de, 2026-09 boostroom.com, 2026-02.
  • Confusing item level with combat power and misjudging whether you can actually clear a dungeon confirmed, 2+ sources expcarry.com, 2026-08 mein-mmo.de, 2026-09.
  • Unlocking a skill effect but forgetting to manually slot it, the effect does not apply until assigned single source mmoexp.com, date unknown.
    Avoid: assuming an unlocked skill effect is automatically active, check your skill slots.
  • Spreading Skill Points and Stigma resources across too many builds instead of committing to one coherent PvE or PvP setup first confirmed, 2+ sources mein-mmo.de, 2026-09 boostroom.com, 2026-02.
  • Not tracking your own progress or metrics, which makes it hard to tell whether your routine is actually working versus the game simply being grindy single source mmoexp.com, date unknown.
