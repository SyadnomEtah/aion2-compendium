Gladiator

Role: Melee DPS with off-tank flex. Difficulty: Forgiving to learn, but early levels can feel sluggish. Weapon: Greatsword or Polearm.

DPSMeleeOff-tank capable

Overview and role

confirmed kr-era

Gladiator is a forgiving, self-sustaining melee DPS that can flex into off-tank duty.

The Gladiator is AION 2's heavy melee DPS with meaningful off-tank capability, wielding two-handed weapons: a Greatsword or a Polearm confirmed aion2hub, date unknown mmom, date unknown.

In Korean the class is referred to as 검성, roughly "sword saint." Korean community threads on Inven's 검성 board match the Gladiator's kit and confirm the KR name mapping single source inven, date unknown.

Kit size

AION2 Hub lists the kit at 52 total skills, 43 active and 9 passive single source aion2hub classes, date unknown; a separate fetch of the class page gave 40 active/9 passive for the same 52 total contested on the active count, same domain aion2hub, date unknown.

Role and difficulty

  • Pitched as one of the more forgiving melee classes to learn, thanks to a simple, rhythm-based playstyle built around basic attacks and lifesteal sustain rather than tight execution windows single source redwendy, date unknown
  • High HP pool lets it flex into an off-tank/secondary-tank role in group content even though its primary job is frontline DPS confirmed aion2hub, date unknown

PvE standing (KR/TW meta): strong for dungeons and boss fights via sustained AoE damage and self-healing, and can function as a sub-tank when geared for it single source ssegold, date unknown.

KR-era, pre-launch feedback: focus-group testers reportedly found the class "slow and clunky" with skills that "felt weak and lacked impact" prior to a rebalance pass, and multiple sources agree the class "transforms" once mid-game gear, animations, and cooldown mastery come online contested-but-consistent, two independent sources fextralife, date unknown lagofast, 2026. Balance patches may have already addressed this by TW/global launch.

PvP and party standing

  • Excels in frontline skirmishes and small-scale fights (1v1, 2v2) thanks to burst damage, mobility, and CC tools, with success depending heavily on timing and positioning rather than pure stats single source mmoexp, date unknown
  • LepreStore's role-based tier framing places Gladiator as combining "strong PvP with durability and useful party buffs" rather than ranking it in a strict S-F tier single source leprestore, 2026

Party demand: solid as a melee DPS that can flex to off-tank duty in a pinch, though no source gave a hard queue-priority figure.

Best for players who want a durable, self-sustaining melee class that rewards positioning and cluster-hitting over precise combo execution, and who want a class that is comparatively forgiving for solo leveling single source u4n, date unknown.

Skill priority

contested kr-era

Two KR guides partly disagree; Attack Preparation is the one passive every source agrees is critical.

Two independent Vortex Gaming write-ups give partially overlapping, partially conflicting PvE skill priorities. Both are KR-era.

Priority order 1

  1. Do first: Strike of Ruin (Specialized)
  2. Then: Charge Strike, Shatter Wave, Ankle Cut, Leap Strike
  3. Later: Armor of Balance, Zikel's Blessing, Assault Stance

vortexgaming, date unknown

Priority order 2

  1. Do first: Survival Stance, called strong for both PvP and PvE
  2. Then: Sharp Separation (three specializations, or spirit/MP recovery if mana is tight), Slam of Severance (movement-skill change plus critical-hit MP recovery)
  3. Then: Leap Strike (combat-readiness effect matters, recast reset useful for grinding), Strike Down (specialize for action-immune targets, guarantees a critical hit at skill level 20)
  4. Then: Crushing Wave, recast reset on critical hit rated essential
  5. Deprioritize: Armor of Protection and Damage Absorption, citing low efficiency from long recast times

vortexgaming, date unknown

Core masteries (Korean guidance)

  • 예리한 일격 (Sharp Strike / Keen Strike): non-targeted, move-while-casting hit that builds threat and restores MP; level to 12
  • 절단의 맹타 (Cleaving Fierce Strike): AoE hit that raises threat and the Groggy gauge; also level to 12 single source redwendy, date unknown

Passives

  • Attack Preparation (공격 준비, raises Attack Power and Defense): repeatedly called the single most important passive across both English and Korean sources confirmed vortexgaming, date unknown redwendy, date unknown
  • Veteran Counter: amplifies damage after a successful block; recommended to be maxed out single source vortexgaming, date unknown
  • Weakness Analysis (약점 파악, raises critical hit rate and Perfect stat) and Blood Absorption (피의 흡수, lifesteal on hit) round out the recommended passive set single source redwendy, date unknown

Skip and chain notes

Skills to ignore early: Armor of Protection and Damage Absorption per priority order 2 above; treat as single-source/contested since priority order 1 does not mention either skill.

Mastery/chain skills: Strike Down is a chain enabler against action-immune (staggered) targets and a guaranteed-crit finisher once specialized to level 20 single source vortexgaming, date unknown.

How the board works

Daevanion Points buy nodes on a shared board; nodes add either flat combat stats or extra levels to specific skills. Points come from normal leveling plus Daevanion Crystals from quests and achievements.

Strategy: path to the nearest node that actually helps your loadout and treat filler connector nodes as a toll, not a destination. Damage-focused builds like Gladiator's should weight uptime and output nodes over pure survival nodes: "damage builds value uptime and output... tanks value control and survival" expcarry, date unknown.

kr-era Reset costs 500 Kinah per individual node in the Korean client; cost may differ at global launch expcarry, date unknown. No reliable source found on when specifically a Gladiator should reset.

  • Chalice: Vitality Chalice recommended, prioritize the Strike Down and Crushing Wave slots.

  • Compass: Slam of Severance and Crushing Wave essential, followed by Violate Sword, Dashing Strike, Sword Aura Tree, and Impact Release.

Separately, on the newer Arcana set additions, the 수호 (Guardian) and 불굴 (Indomitable) sets are framed as PvP-oriented and 징벌 (Punishment) as PvE-oriented, implying a Gladiator PvE build should avoid over-investing in the Guardian/Indomitable sets kr-era single source vortexgaming, date unknown.

Rotation and combos

confirmed pattern kr-era

Open with Assault Stance, chain AoE into finishers, and save lifesteal skills for right after big hits.

Beginner PvE rotation, synthesizing the two Vortex Gaming skill-priority lists:

  1. Open with Assault Stance for the combat-speed and MP-cost buff.
  2. Weave in Sharp Strike / 예리한 일격 periodically to keep MP topped up between bigger hits.
  3. Lead with your AoE knockdown, Charge Strike, Path Explosion, or Shatter Wave depending on which build you're following, to group and stagger enemies.
  4. Follow with single-target finishers, Strike Down, Ankle Cut, Leap Strike, on priority or action-immune targets.
  5. Close with Crushing Wave once its recast has been reset by a critical hit confirmed pattern vortexgaming, date unknown vortexgaming, date unknown.

Key chain combos: Forced Bind into Downward Strike as a PvP setup-into-burst combo, and Path Explosion (knockdown) into Downward Strike as an alternate opener when Forced Bind is unavailable. Blade Toss doubles as a farming or poke damage tool outside of combo windows single source vortexgaming, date unknown.

Macro setup documented for auto-hunt or quick execution: a macro bound to Blade of Severance, with Sharp Strike, Sword Dance, Path Explosion, and Downward Strike all linked to a single left-click input for streamlined execution single source vortexgaming, date unknown.

Defensive and utility cooldowns

Blood Smash (or "Lifestealing Blade" in another source's naming) converts roughly 15% of damage dealt into HP. It's recommended as a reactive tool, used right after taking a big hit or during a burst trade rather than spammed on cooldown, since its value comes from timing, not uptime single source pvpbank, date unknown.

Korean community advice reinforces this for AoE survival specifically: dropping 분쇄파동 (Shatter/Crushing Wave) into the center of a mob cluster heals you sharply in proportion to how many enemies it hits, a panic-button sustain tool when surrounded single source inven, date unknown.

Leveling notes

confirmed marketing

Gladiator levels fast and safe: strong AoE clear plus lifesteal sustain, though early levels feel slow.

Solo leveling comfort: high. Multiple independent sources describe Gladiator as one of the most stable and forgiving melee classes to solo-level, citing consistent AoE clearing plus reliable lifesteal sustain confirmed u4n, date unknown u4gm, date unknown.

Sustain is primarily lifesteal-based: the Blood Absorption passive plus active skills like Blood Smash/Lifestealing Blade convert a percentage of damage into HP, rather than relying on potions or mana regen.

Sustain scales with landing hits on multiple enemies at once, so positioning for cluster hits directly improves survivability confirmed, same sources.

Mob density tips: the class is explicitly built to wade into mob clusters and clear them with sweeping AoE while healing off the damage dealt; learning enemy spawn and movement patterns to strike during cluster moments is called out as the core solo-leveling skill for this class single source u4gm, date unknown.

KR-era, pre-launch feedback: early levels reportedly feel sluggish and low-impact based on focus-test feedback, but the class is consistently described as transforming in the mid-game once better animations, tighter combos, and cooldown mastery come online contested-but-consistent fextralife, date unknown mmoexp, date unknown. Later balance passes may have changed this.

Common mistakes

mostly single source marketing

Learn to dodge, weave basic attacks for MP, and enhance your weapon before armor.

  1. Assuming heavy armor lets you tank hits without needing to dodge; learning dodge timing is called out as one of the biggest power spikes a new Gladiator player can unlock single source aion2wiki, date unknown.
  2. Poor MP management from spamming skills instead of weaving basic attacks and Sharp Strike (예리한 일격) to keep mana topped up confirmed, English and Korean sources redwendy, date unknown inven, date unknown.
  3. Over-investing scarce upgrade materials into starting gear before learning core combat and movement fundamentals single source aion2wiki, date unknown.
  4. Not prioritizing the weapon slot for enhancement, when weapon level drives most of a DPS class's damage output single source, same guide aion2wiki, date unknown.
  5. Spamming lifesteal skills (Blood Smash/Lifestealing Blade) on cooldown instead of saving them for immediately after a big hit or during a burst trade, which wastes their sustain value single source pvpbank, date unknown.
  6. Ignoring positioning for AoE cluster hits, not baiting or gathering mobs into cleave range before committing an AoE skill single source u4gm, date unknown.
  7. Neglecting the Attack Preparation and Veteran Counter passives, which multiple sources call the class's most important scaling passives, in favor of leveling only active skills confirmed vortexgaming, date unknown redwendy, date unknown.
  8. Picking Greatsword or Polearm without matching the choice to intended content (Greatsword for PvE stability, Polearm for PvP reach/burst), then being surprised the weapon doesn't fit the playstyle single source u4gm, date unknown.
  9. Investing Stigma Shards evenly across many Stigmas instead of maxing the two or three actually equipped first, diluting overall power expcarry, date unknown.
    • The two or three actually equipped are Assault Stance and Zikel's Blessing.
    • Inferred from the general Stigma spend-priority advice, applied to the Gladiator-specific picks documented above.
