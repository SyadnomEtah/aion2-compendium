Leveling and priorities

A level-by-level map of what unlocks when, how fast you can expect to level, and when to stop rushing the story and go fix a power problem instead.

Level milestones

confirmed where noted

Daevanion Boards open roughly every 10 levels; Stigma slots unlock at level 23.

  1. Lv 12
    First Daevanion Board: Nezekan unlocks

    Daevanion Boards spend "Daevanion Points" on stat and skill nodes. Nezekan is the first board fextralife.com, date unknown.

  2. Lv 20
    Zikel board unlocks

    Second Daevanion Board fextralife.com, date unknown. contested a separate search summary lists a longer 8-board ladder naming "Marchutan and Yustiel" instead of this 6-board list, unverified directly against the wiki.

  3. Lv 23
    Stigma system unlocks

    Up to 4 equippable skill-enhancement slots. Stigma Shards (not normal skill points) power these and can reportedly be bought from an Abyss Exchange Shop with Abyss Points aion2hub.com, date unknown boostroom.com, date unknown.

  4. Lv 30
    Vaizel board unlocks

    Third Daevanion Board fextralife.com, date unknown.

  5. Lv 40
    Triniel board unlocks

    Fourth Daevanion Board fextralife.com, date unknown.

  6. Lv 45 (KR-era cap)
    Ariel and Azphel boards unlock

    Final two boards in the 6-board wiki list.

    Node costs across all boards: grey/stat nodes cost 1 point, green/passive skill nodes 2, blue/active skill nodes 3, orange/unique skill nodes 4 fextralife.com, date unknown. Guides recommend roughly item level 1,000 and enough combat power to be safe by this point. single source, kr-era numbers mein-mmo.de, 2026-09

  7. Any level
    Active skill thresholds: 8, 12, 16

    Active skills reportedly need to reach skill level 8, 12, or 16 to unlock stronger effects; guides warn that lagging behind these thresholds makes damage feel weak. single source, lower-trust overgear.com, date unknown

  8. Endgame
    Item level 1,500 for the Ascension Trial

    The Ascension Trial (Forgotten Repository) should wait until roughly item level 1,500 to avoid wasting a weekly attempt. single source, kr-era mein-mmo.de, 2026-09

Numbers vary between sources and are flagged with confidence tags above. Also see the Asmodian equivalent leveling guide aion2hub.com, date unknown, and further Stigma/Daevanion skill progression guides expcarry.com, date unknown mmoexp.com, date unknown.

First day

confirmed

Follow Main Story quests first, use Region quests as a supplement, do not race.

General consensus across guides: follow the Main Story (Episode) Quests first, use nearby Region quests as a supplement, and treat the first days as a sequence of unlocks rather than a race.

Episode quests carry the most XP and gate which systems, zones, and mounts become available; Region quests give lower XP but extra gear and Daevanion Crystals, unlocking once you meet their level requirement. exitlag.com, date unknown aion2wiki.com, date unknown

  1. Do first: push Episode/Main Story quests, days 1 to 2. Campaign focus and early system unlocks single source expcarry.com, 2026-08.
  2. Then: pick up Region quests as they unlock for extra gear and Daevanion Crystals.
  3. Later / optional: do not chase every side system the moment it appears, let the story pace your unlocks.

First week

single source

Days 1-2 campaign, days 3-4 gear and first dungeons, days 5-7 settle into a routine.

One guide's suggested first-week phase breakdown: Days 1 to 2, campaign focus and system unlocks; Days 3 to 4, gear reinforcement and first dungeons; Days 5 to 7, settling into a sustainable daily/weekly routine. expcarry.com, 2026-08

  1. Do first: days 3 to 4, reinforce gear and clear your first dungeons once the story opens them up.
  2. Then: days 5 to 7, settle into a sustainable daily and weekly routine rather than continuing to rush. See the Systems and Checklists panel for the actual daily/weekly task list.
  3. Later / optional: if you are also doing side content beyond MSQ, expect this week to stretch a bit longer, see pacing below.

When to stop rushing and fix power walls

confirmed

Push the story until you hit a genuine wall, then fix that one bottleneck before moving on.

The recurring advice across multiple guides: push the main story until you hit a genuine power wall, a fight or dungeon you cannot clear, then stop and address that specific bottleneck (gear, a skill threshold, or a missing system) rather than grinding broadly or opening every menu as soon as it appears. expcarry.com, 2026-08 aion2wiki.com, kr-era

Beginner tip: "Gear Score" is community slang. The game itself uses item level (gear-only, gates dungeon entry) and combat power (a broader number including Daevanion talents, Pantheon unlocks, Arcana cards, and skill levels). Do not confuse the two: a dungeon's stated item-level minimum can still be a hard failure if your combat power isn't there yet. expcarry.com, 2026-08 mein-mmo.de, 2026-09
  1. Do first: when you hit a wall, diagnose it, is it gear (item level), build (combat power/skill thresholds), or a missing system, before grinding blindly.
  2. Then: fix that specific bottleneck rather than broadly farming XP or opening unrelated systems.
  3. Later / optional: only return to open-ended grinding once the wall is cleared and you are looking for general progress.

Time to cap and pacing

single source

Budget 20-25 hours to cap if you touch side content; a rushed 12-15 hour MSQ run is weaker.

Experienced players following only the MSQ reportedly hit level 45 in about 12 to 15 hours; new players who also do side content should expect roughly 20 to 25 hours. expcarry.com, date unknown

That extra time is described as producing a "significantly more functional" character at cap, with more Daevanion points and gear investment, than rushing. unverified

Spot-check 2026-09-12: the cited page argues MSQ-first but contains no hour figures, so the 12 to 15 and 20 to 25 hour numbers are an uncorroborated estimate.

Beginner tip: unless you specifically want a speed-run, budget for the slower 20 to 25 hour pace. A more "functional" character at cap matters more than shaving a few hours off leveling.
