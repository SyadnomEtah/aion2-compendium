Cleric

Role: Pure healer. Difficulty: Low to Medium. Weapon: Mace + Shield/Guard (Staff option for a DPS lean).

HealerSupportBeginner friendlyNear-mandatory in groups

Overview and role

confirmed kr-era

Cleric is a near-mandatory pure healer: tanky, solo-friendly, but its tier likely dropped after a KR/TW patch.

Version note: AION 2 has been live in KR/TW since November 2025. Global PC launch is October 5, 2026, with a fully wiped Launch Scale Test on September 17-18 that does not carry over. Most Cleric guides below are KR/TW-era. A reported August 2026 balance patch cut fixed healing passives in favor of attack-power-scaling healing, reportedly pushing Cleric toward C-tier single source leprestore, date unknown. Treat any older "S-tier healer" claim as stale.

Role and kit

Cleric wields a Mace paired with a shield or magic offhand ("Guard") for a tankier build, with a Staff option for higher magic boost and more damage oneall forum, date unknown.

AION2 Hub describes it as a back-line healer who cycles AoE and over-time heals plus protective shields to sustain the group aion2hub, date unknown.

  • Only class that can actively dispel debuffs, and carries the signature Summon Resurrection skill, making it near-mandatory for dungeon groups confirmed, multiple sources aion2hub, date unknown fextralife, date unknown
  • Fextralife calls it "an indispensable character in party play," but says it specializes in AoE healing over single-target: "the Cleric's healing alone will not be enough" if the party ignores mechanics fextralife, date unknown

Standing by mode

  • Party demand: very high, near-mandatory for dungeons and raids
  • PvE: strong utility pick from its heal, shield, cleanse and res kit, though the August 2026 patch reportedly weakened fixed healing passives marketing source leprestore, date unknown
  • PvP: viable as a "survival healer" build, but reportedly slipped to C-tier alongside Chanter after the same patch single source leprestore, date unknown
  • Solo: good. Fextralife calls it "accessible for solo players"; other guides call it tanky and safe to solo, kill speed aside, thanks to self-sustain from its own healing kit fextralife, date unknown mmoexp, date unknown
Beginner tip: pick Cleric if you want a forgiving, hard-to-kill class with guaranteed group invites and don't mind managing cooldown rhythm and positioning over raw damage numbers.

Skill priority

contested in detail kr-era

Keep a heal and a damage skill both leveled; the exact global-launch order is unconfirmed, so treat lists below as directional.

Gap: no reliable source found for a precise, level-by-level global-launch skill point order. The lists below are pieced together from KR/TW-era guides and should be treated as directional, not exact.
  1. Do first: keep 1-2 healing skills (Healing Light / Light of Regeneration) leveled alongside your main damage chain.
    • A Cleric with no heal rank is unplayable in groups, and one with no damage rank kills too slowly solo (synthesized from the sources below).
  2. Do first: below level 20, spec Divine Retribution 2 and 4 to leverage the spirit synergy vortexgaming, date unknown
  3. Then (PvE, level 20+): Divine Retribution (2/3/4), Judgment Bolt (2/4/5), Sacred Energy (3/4/5), Light of Regeneration (1/4/5), Troops (2/3/4) vortexgaming, date unknown
    • Sacred Energy is a higher DPS priority; Troops hits max DPS with a full charge.
  4. Then (PvP): shift Light of Regeneration to 1/2/5 (defense over mobility), Troops to 3/4 (full-charge windows are rarer in PvP), and Shock Release to 1/4 or 1/5 at level 16+ vortexgaming, date unknown
  5. Later / optional: a separate arcana-tied guide orders leveling as Forge first (highest DPS), then Swift Heal, Earth's Retribution, Rain of Healing, Troops, and Lightning Judgment vortexgaming, date unknown
    • That guide suggests reducing Light of Regeneration below level 16 to free points.

Named kits

AION2 Hub names the offensive kit as Earth's Retribution, Judgment Thunder, and Divine Punishment, and the healing kit as Light of Regeneration, Healing Light, and Radiant Recovery (the grouping is this guide's; the source lists the skills without grouping them) aion2hub, date unknown.

Playstyle: weaving damage skills between healing cycles to maintain mana efficiency. A basic solo leveling loop cited elsewhere is applying Punishment Seal, spamming Holy Strike, and healing as needed marketing, lower trust.

Stigma picks

contested kr-era

Level Earth's Punishment to 20 first; beyond that, two guides disagree on names, and unlock-level claims are single-source.

What stigmas are

Stigmas are advanced skills separate from your leveling tree, funded with Stigma Shards instead of Skill Points, equipped in a limited number of slots (6 in the KR-era cap) expcarry, date unknown.

  • A general AION 2 stigma-system explainer: complete a level-20 campaign quest to begin using Stigma Stones single source boostroom, date unknown
    • Slots open automatically at further level thresholds.
    • This source is written specifically about AION 2, not the original Aion, though it doesn't caveat KR-versus-global differences.
  • A weaker, Korean community signal says two additional advanced stigma slots open at level 45. This is a slot-count claim only, and the source thread could not be isolated, so treat it as unconfirmed single source, weakly sourced

Level 45 advanced stigma list (Korean Cleric board)

A Korean Cleric-board thread on 상급스티그마 선택 (advanced stigma selection) recommends this set, all to level 25 unless noted single source kr-era inven, date unknown:

  • 대지의징벌 (Earth's Punishment)
  • 보호의빛 (Protection's Light)
  • 고결한기운 (Noble Energy), scales better for damage at levels 20-25 than Amplified Prayer
  • 증폭의기도 (Amplified Prayer), to level 23 only, whose main benefit is a 1.5-second duration increase

Resets

Individual nodes or the whole board can reportedly be reset, at a KR-era cost of about 500 Kinah per node, explicitly flagged as "subject to change on Global" expcarry, date unknown.

General guidance: build one full rotation on Mastery skills first, fund the Stigmas used in that rotation, trace the cheapest Daevanion path that supports the same build, and only reset when your role changes meaningfully.

Daevanion Points and Crystals come from leveling/quests, achievements, and content activities expcarry, date unknown.

Arcana

single source kr-era

Fill Chalice with damage/heal skills, prioritize Divine Grace on Bell and Healing Power Boost on Mirror, and lock old cards instead of deleting them.

Card types

Arcana cards come in types referenced as Chalice, Bell, Mirror, and Scales vortexgaming, date unknown:

  • Chalice: aim for 2+ of these skills on one card - Forge, Swift Heal, Earth's Retribution, Rain of Healing, Troops, Lightning Judgment vortexgaming, date unknown
  • Bell: priority order is Divine Grace (minimum level 3), then Shield of Immortality, then Warm Aura
  • Mirror: priority is Healing Power Boost first (target level 4), then Earth's Blessing second (target level 3), which improves critical-damage scaling
  • Scales: get at least 2 useful active skills on the card since it "heavily influences your overall setup direction"

A separate AI-summarized search result for the same underlying guide family gives a near-identical Bell/Mirror/Scales list, cross-confirming the above.

Setup and cost strategy

When synthesizing Chalice cards, check 2 roll options if affordable; for other card types, 1 option is enough to save resources. For general bonus sets, favor "Frenzy 4 Pure" or "History 4 Pure" over "Vitality," since Vitality cards are expensive and not worth chasing unless already owned.

Avoid: deleting old Arcana cards when the meta shifts. Lock them instead, since balance patches can bring old cards back into relevance. Don't sink heavy resources into Vitality-type cards from scratch vortexgaming, date unknown.

Rotation and combos

single source marketing

Solo, apply Punishment Seal and spam Holy Strike; in a party, cycle heals and shields proactively rather than firefighting damage.

Solo PvE loop

  1. Apply Punishment Seal.
  2. Spam Holy Strike as your main damage filler.
  3. Heal as needed rather than on a fixed timer.

Earth's Retribution and Judgment Thunder let Cleric chip in meaningful ranged damage and sustain its own mana while healing aion2hub, date unknown.

Party and support rhythm

Cleric does not spam heals constantly; it follows a rhythm confirmed, qualitative: cycling AoE and over-time heals, layering shields proactively, and cleansing debuffs rather than firefighting single-target damage.

Fextralife: the Cleric specializes in Area of Effect healing rather than single-target support, and gameplay is bouncing between different healing sources while weaving in damaging skills fextralife, date unknown.

Many heals (e.g. Healing Light) can be charged for a stronger effect at the cost of higher MP and cast time.

Beginner tip: use shield skills like Immortal Curtain (absorbs a fixed amount for up to 10 seconds per Fextralife) proactively before a known boss mechanic hits, not reactively after damage lands.

Macro layout

Suggested layout: Charge on Q, all three healing skills bound together on Num 1, instant attack skills on Num 2, Stigma skills spread across Num 5-8 keyran, date unknown.

The same source cautions that most veteran players keep healing and buff macros manual rather than fully macroed, since heal/buff timing precision matters more than raw damage-chain speed.

Leveling notes

single source marketing

Cleric levels safely and comfortably solo thanks to self-healing, but kills slower than true DPS classes.

Solo leveling comfort rates positively across sources: Cleric is "tanky and safe to play solo" and, unlike a stereotypical helpless solo healer, has enough offensive tools to handle solo content while maintaining excellent sustain marketing, lower trust u4n, date unknown.

Its self-heal (e.g. Healing Light) means less time on potions or downtime between fights.

Kill speed is slower than true DPS classes; the tradeoff is durability and near-zero death risk while leveling.

Gap: no reliable source found with specific pull-size or mob-density numbers for Cleric, and none identifying a specific level or gear milestone where Cleric's power spikes. General guides only say the class stays viable and safe throughout leveling.

Common mistakes

marketing

Stay active and mobile: shield before damage lands, swap Stigmas by content, and don't lean on burst heals to fix mechanics.

Do

  • Apply offensive pressure during downtime instead of only reacting to damage mmoexp, date unknown
  • Call out repositioning so AoE heals land on a grouped party
  • Use shields proactively before a known burst ("Immortal Curtain is proactive, not reactive")
  • Balance Skill Points between damage and healing kits rather than dumping into one
  • Swap Stigma loadouts by content (PvE party, PvE solo, PvP), per Korean community guidance ("don't stick to one setup, must swap by content")
  • Lock old Arcana cards instead of deleting them when the meta shifts vortexgaming, date unknown
  • Stay mobile while healing to dodge avoidable boss/PvP attacks

Do not

  • Play as a purely passive, reactive healer mmoexp, date unknown
  • Heal when nobody needs it, wasting MP and triggering long cooldowns early in a fight
  • Assume AoE heals land when the party is spread out too far
  • Rely on burst single-target healing to fix every mechanic mistake, "the Cleric's healing alone will not be enough" fextralife, date unknown
  • Use shields reactively after damage already landed
  • Stand still while healing instead of staying mobile
