Glossary

System names, currencies, and jargon from the Systems guide confirmed
Mastery Skill
Your class's core combat ability, learned automatically at level requirements and raised with Skill Points up to a base cap of 10. expcarry, date unknown
Specialization
Chosen bonus effects a skill gains at level breakpoints 8, 12, and 16, up to 3 extra effects per skill. Fextralife, date unknown
Empyrean Trace / Feather
A world collectible turned in at a Monolith for Wisdom Stones. Naming is inconsistent between sources, possibly different translations of the same KR item. Fextralife, date unknown Differ, date unknown
Wisdom Stone
A consumable obtained by exchanging Empyrean Traces/Feathers at a Monolith, grants Skill Points on use. Fextralife, date unknown
Stigma
An equippable skill-enhancement stone adding build-defining abilities on top of your class kit. First slot unlocks around level 23. expcarry, date unknown
Stigma Shard
The currency spent to level a Stigma Skill, separate from normal Skill Points. Higher-tier Shards exist for KR levels 21-25. expcarry, date unknown
Daevanion Board
A permanent node-board progression system, separate from gear, raising combat stats and effective skill levels. First board unlocks at level 12. Fextralife, date unknown
Daevanion Point
Spent to unlock individual Daevanion board nodes; earned from regional quests, Sealed Dungeons, Strongholds, achievements, and exploration. Overgear, date unknown
Daevanion Crystal
Earned alongside Daevanion Points from the same sources, also obtainable via material conversion; feeds Daevanion board progression. expcarry, date unknown
Arcana
A card-based system adding Item Level, combat stats, set bonuses, and effective skill levels at once. Slots include Chalice, Parchment, Compass, Bell, and Mirror, later expanded. expcarry, date unknown
Training Arcana
A consumable that feeds experience to Arcana cards; tiers are Lesser (200 EXP), Intermediate (600), Superior (1,500), and Ultimate (3,000). expcarry, date unknown
Mana Stone
A socketed gem added to gear for stats like Crit Rate or Speed, found via dungeons, rewards, or crafting; AION 2's Manastone equivalent. u4n, date unknown
Soul Codex
Alongside Mana Stones, described as helping gear "scale into late-game without major farming" by increasing attribute effects. Fextralife, date unknown
Soul Binding
A secondary stat block on gear that reportedly "only grows once you bind it." Single-source, unverified. kodex.yavuz, date unknown
Theostone
A weapon proc stone, AION 2's Godstone equivalent, socketed into a weapon's dedicated slot for a chance-on-hit effect. One effect per weapon; identical Theostones do not stack. 153 known Theostones cataloged. aion2.online, date unknown
Enhance Stone
Spent to raise a gear piece's enhancement level. Extraction from a previously-enhanced item refunds 100% of the Enhance Stones spent into it. exitlag, date unknown
Broker
AION 2's player marketplace/Auction House; Kinah is the only currency usable there. Fextralife, date unknown
Pantheon
A permanent passive-stat system built from Statues, Colossi, and "Artwork"; placing a Colossus grants points toward a deity domain, each capping at 200 points before converting to flat combat stats. Vortex Gaming, date unknown
Colossus
A Pantheon item slotted to grant domain points; its rarity (Green/Blue/Purple/Gold) determines bonus strength. rsnxt, date unknown
Achievement Points
Granted by completing achievements; ties into Daevanion Point/Crystal income. Differ, date unknown
Title
An earned label from PvP rank, Arena tiers, boss kills, monster-hunting milestones, or events; stat titles grant bonuses like PvP Attack or HP when equipped, some are purely cosmetic. aion2hub, date unknown
Appearance Collection
A wardrobe-style collection; roughly 700 appearances grants combat speed and cooldown reduction as a milestone bonus. Cash-shop cosmetics do not count toward it. Vortex Gaming, date unknown
Soul (Pets/Mounts)
A monster drop; farming 10 souls of almost any monster unlocks it as a pet/mount. Also drops from the Shugo Shop and Black Cloud Merchant. Fextralife, date unknown
Flight Power
A wing's airborne stamina stat, managed via a physics-based "Glide Gauge" rather than a rigid timer. TheGamer, date unknown
Glide Gauge
The physics-based mechanic underlying Flight Power; running it out mid-air drops you. u4n, date unknown
Legion
AION 2's guild system, capped at up to 128 members, used for PvE, PvP, crafting, and social access. 6Sword, date unknown
Legion Hall
Unlocks at Legion level 5, costs 1,000,000 Kinah to rent, houses all five crafting professions' merchants/workbenches together. mmoexp, date unknown
Legion Coin
Earned for active Legion contribution; spent on Legion-exclusive skins, uniforms, masks, mounts, and wings. u4n, date unknown
Accuracy
Governs the chance to land an attack; per one source, also affects crit chance. u4n, date unknown
Precision
A hybrid stat that raises both Accuracy and Crit chance. u4n, date unknown
PvE Attack / Defense
Comes only from PvE gear; only affects damage dealt to and taken from monsters. exitlag, date unknown
PvP Attack / Defense
Comes only from PvP gear; only affects damage dealt to and taken from other players. exitlag, date unknown
Item Level
A gating number for content access, cited around 900-2700+ tiers for Transcendence content. Fextralife, date unknown
Combat Power ("Atul")
An aggregate power estimate used for matchmaking/ranking-style comparisons. The "Atul" name is single-source and may be a translation artifact. u4n, date unknown
Transcendence
AION 2's endgame progression/ranking layer, fed by specific dungeons with up to 4 escalating difficulty levels. aion2hub, 2026-07
Odyle Energy
Spent to open the reward cube after clearing an Expedition or Transcendence dungeon; entry itself is not gated by energy. Normal cube costs 40, Hard costs 60. aion2hub, 2026-07
Essence Extraction
The gathering skill/action used to mine ore, gather herbs, or cut wood, feeding all five crafting professions. expcarry, date unknown
Kinah
The main in-game currency; earned from quests, monster kills, dungeons, and vendor sales, spent on repairs, soul-binding, crafting materials, teleports, and gear enhancement, the only currency usable on the Broker. u4n, date unknown
Quna
The premium/cash-shop currency, obtained mainly via real-money purchase and tradeable for Kinah through a Currency Exchange, spent on cosmetics and Battle Pass premium tiers. u4n, date unknown
Abyss Points
Earned from daily/weekly PvP-zone quests; presumed to buy Abyss-specific gear/items, not independently confirmed. Fextralife, date unknown
Centuryroot Token
Spent at a "Shugo Festival" seasonal shop; purpose beyond that event unverified. Fextralife, date unknown
