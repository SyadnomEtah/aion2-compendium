General routine advice, confirmed across 2+ sources: do quick daily tasks first (5 Duty/Mission quests plus any available dungeon entries that give materials, currency, or progression), then longer weekly content.

Missing a day or two of duty quests will not badly damage progression, but playing with no weekly plan at all is the single biggest beginner mistake. vortexgaming.io, date unknown boostroom.com, 2026-02

Mentor system

10+ level gap kr-era single source

Mentoring costs the mentor nothing but tokens; a mentee gets a free boost if the system exists at launch.

A higher-level player can mentor someone more than 10 levels below them. While mentoring, the mentor gets no XP or drops but the mentee still gets both; the mentor instead earns Circle Tokens (Asmodian) or Orichalcum Tokens (Elyos), exchangeable for gear, pet eggs, titles, and accessories.

This appeared in KR patch notes ("Chapter 1") alongside a Membership Event Pass and mentee onboarding track, and may not exist at global launch day one. daevasreport.com, date unknown

Beginner tip: if the mentor system is live at global launch, a mentee benefits without any downside, worth asking a higher-level Legion mate for a mentor slot.
