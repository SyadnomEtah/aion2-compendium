More systems worth knowing

Smaller systems that sit outside the core progression cards but are still worth knowing about. Most of the other systems players ask about are covered on other pages of this guide.

Legion mechanical perks are contested (three independent sources say levels, warehouse and buffs do not exist), and several candidate systems were not found in any AION 2-specific source. The full coverage table is the last card below.

Character Creation and Customization

Runs right after the opening tutorial, before level 1 confirmed official

Character creation runs before level 1 with 200+ sliders and presets, but changing your look later needs an unclear cash-shop item.

What it is

Character creation runs after the opening combat tutorial and story introduction, covering body type, face, hair, and body-shape sliders plus ready-made presets NCSoft official, 2025-11-16.

NCSoft's own figure is "more than 200 customization options" spanning body shape, skin tone, eye color, and muscle definition NCSoft official, 2025-11-16.

How it works

  • Four sections: body type, face, hair and makeup, and body shape Fextralife, date unknown timesaver.gg, date unknown
    • Body type: masculine frames lean armor-like, feminine frames lean dresses/skirts/heels.
    • Face: eyebrows, eyes, iris, nose, lips.
    • Hair and makeup: available regardless of body type.
    • Body shape: height, limb proportions, muscularity, regional thickness.
  • Ready-made presets are organized by race, gender, and role so you can skip detailed sculpting timesaver.gg, date unknown
  • Faction, class, and race are treated as permanent, with no swap service announced for global as of 2026-09-12 timesaver.gg, date unknown

Once creation is finished, you cannot change your physical appearance without "a special kind of currency," per the community wiki, which does not name it Fextralife, date unknown.

A second source frames this as "likely possible through a cash-shop restyle item" but says NCSoft has not published a specific item, currency, or global price single source, speculative timesaver.gg, date unknown.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: use a preset close to what you want rather than free-sculpting from scratch if you won't spend real time on it, since changing your look later needs a currency or item that isn't clearly defined yet for global.

Watch out

Avoid: assuming you can freely re-roll your face later the way some other MMOs allow. Budget your creation-screen time up front.

Housing

Not yet live on KR/TW as of 2026-09-12 single source kr-era, unreleased

Player housing is confirmed and in development, but not live yet on KR/TW and not guaranteed for global day one.

What it is

A confirmed, in-development player housing system with exterior decoration (fences, statues, decorations) and interior furniture placement mmoexp, 2026-07-28.

Pets and mounts are shown living inside and around houses, consistent with AION 2's unified pet/mount system mmoexp, 2026-07-28.

How it works

  • Status as of 2026-07-28: "actively in development." mmoexp, 2026-07-28
    • Expected on KR/TW "within the next few months as part of early chapter updates."
    • Global housing is expected "at or shortly after" the October 5, 2026 worldwide release. NCSoft has not confirmed exact dates, unlock requirements, or costs.
  • Until housing ships, the Pantheon system already gives a private space to place "trophies and artwork" for stat bonuses, but without free-form furniture placement, per the same article

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: don't hold back on Pantheon investment while waiting for housing, they're described as separate systems, and Pantheon is confirmed live now while housing is not.

Watch out

Avoid: assuming housing is available at global launch day one. Treat it as likely but not confirmed for day one.

Accessories: Class Runes, Belts, and Amulets

Unlock gate reported but not confirmed confirmed kr-era

Two good Runes are worth about 80 item levels, but stop enhancing at +1, a failed +2 attempt can break the rune.

What it is

A dedicated accessory slot type distinct from Manastones, Theostones, and Arcana cards. Named examples include Clash Rune and Devotion Rune Fextralife, date unknown.

Runes are classed as accessories, obtained through quest completion, exploration, enemy drops, crafting, and merchant purchase, same as other accessory gear Fextralife, date unknown.

How it works

  • Equipping two good runes is worth roughly 80 item levels, corroborated by two independent sources with nearly identical wording HackQuest/mmoexp, date unknown kodex.yavuz, date unknown
  • Enhancement warning: stop rune enhancement at +1. From +2 onward, a failed attempt can break the rune entirely single source kodex.yavuz, date unknown
  • A dedicated video guide titled "How to Unlock the Rune Accessory Slot" implies runes aren't available from level 1 and need a specific quest or milestone; the exact requirement is unconfirmed single source, title only YouTube, date unknown
  • This guide's Combat Power card already names the Noble Belt and Revelation Amulet as early permanent item-level gains. No further independent detail on Belts or Amulets as their own mechanical system was found beyond being accessory gear slots.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: treat the Rune slot as a big, cheap power spike once unlocked, two decent runes are worth roughly a full enhancement tier's worth of item level.

Watch out

Avoid: pushing rune enhancement past +1 before confirming whether global carries the same break-on-fail risk as KR.

Transfer Crafting

Unconfirmed beyond a single source single source kr-era

One source describes a crafting process that carries enhancement progress into an upgraded item, but it's unconfirmed elsewhere.

What it is

A crafting process, distinct from normal Enhance Stone enchanting, that lets a player carry a gear piece's enhancement level, amplification, and Soul Binding progress forward into its next-tier upgrade rather than starting over kodex.yavuz, date unknown.

How it works

  • The same source states the process is "guaranteed to succeed," unlike normal enhancement rolls, and that the resulting upgraded item becomes Soul Bound to the crafter as part of the transfer.
Not corroborated by any second source. Treat the existence, exact mechanics, and required materials as unconfirmed until seen elsewhere. This source is already flagged elsewhere in this guide as one whose single-sourced terms should be treated cautiously.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: if this system exists as described, it removes the fear of "wasting" enhancement investment when upgrading to a better base item. Until corroborated, don't plan your enhancement spending around the assumption that progress definitely transfers.

Legion: Rank Structure and the Warehouse/Buff Controversy

Contested Legion perks contested

Three independent sources say Legions have no warehouse, buffs, or levels, contradicting several community guides.

What it is

Newly confirmed: Legions have a Legion Master and an officer corps; only an officer can rent the Legion hideout, at 1,000,000 Kinah for 23 days and 15 hours, matching the 1,000,000 Kinah rent figure in the Legion card above and adding the exact duration guildmanager.app, date unknown.

The same source states it is explicitly not confirmed how many rank tiers exist, whether rank names can be customized, or what a full permission matrix contains, despite some guides presenting specific rank ladders as fact.

How it works

  • That source states plainly that Legions have "no levels, no buffs, no crafting, no rankings, no warehouse." guildmanager.app, date unknown
    • Attributed to NCSoft developers.
    • It calls out that guides describing Legion warehouses, buffs, and Legion-unlocked Fortress Sieges draw on "an AI-generated page" that invented those features, citing a developer denial dated February 2026.
  • A second, independent source agrees: AION 2's developers "have publicly discussed Legions as intentionally limited." BoostRoom, date unknown
    • No heavy Legion leveling or special buff trees as a core requirement.
    • It explicitly lists Legion levels, warehouses, stat buffs, and crafting bonuses as absent.
  • A third source repeats the no-warehouse claim, also attributed to NCSoft developer commentary without a direct quote or link guildmanager.app, date unknown

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: don't join a Legion expecting a warehouse, guild-wide buffs, or a leveling structure that unlocks more perks over time. Do still join one for the confirmed, undisputed benefits: coordinated scheduling, reliable groups, and the hideout itself once an officer rents it.

Watch out

Direct conflict: the Legion card above states, from a single boosting-adjacent source, that "Legion Hall unlocks at Legion level 5," houses five crafting professions' stations, and that "Legion levels beyond 5 raise the member cap and unlock member access to purchase or use new Abyss weapons and armor." Three independent sources dispute Legion levels, warehouses, and mechanical buffs entirely, and one names "an AI-generated page" as the likely origin of exactly this kind of fabricated claim. Treat the Legion Hall level-5, crafting-unlock and Legion-Coin claims as contested rather than confirmed.
Avoid: picking a Legion based on advertised "Legion level" or "Legion buffs" claims that may not exist in the shipped game.

PvP Ranking Tiers and Season Leaderboards

20-tier seasonal ladder, KR/TW server data single source kr-era

A 20-tier seasonal PvP ladder sits on top of Abyss Points; the gap between low and top tiers is enormous, don't chase rank casually.

What it is

A seasonal ranking ladder layers on top of the Abyss Points system described in the Dungeons section.

One fan database documents 20 tiers for a completed KR/TW season, from Trainee (unranked) up through Ninth- through First-Class Soldier, One-Star through Five-Star Officer, General, Marshal, Commander, and a single Supreme Commander slot for the server's top player aion2hub, date unknown.

How it works

  • Concrete point thresholds: Ninth-Class Soldier requires 3,000 ranking points, while the single Supreme Commander slot requires 25,000,000 aion2hub, date unknown
  • Tiered rewards are listed as titles, statues, pets, coins, and class-specific gear varying by rank and class; the exact reward table per tier was not found
  • A separate, related page tracks Abyss race-ranking rewards for holding Artifacts and topping Abyss world-boss damage/participation, distinct from the general season ladder aion2hub, date unknown
  • The Titles card notes titles come partly "via PvP rank, Arena tiers."
    • It doesn't describe the ladder itself.
    • The Dungeons page covers the Abyss zone, Arenas, and the contested status of Fortress Sieges as activities, but not this seasonal structure.
  • One source describes the Abyss zone as "the central inter-server Abyss battleground," implying cross-server matchmaking or population-sharing, not independently corroborated single source u4n, date unknown
  • Two unverified third-party live-tracking tools exist for current standings: Shugo.gg and AionFlex, neither an official source

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: don't chase rank early. The point gap between low and top tiers (3,000 vs 25,000,000) is enormous and clearly meant for dedicated, server-leading PvP players; casual PvP for gear and Abyss Points doesn't require caring about season rank at all.

Pet Power: Genus Insight and the Possession Effect

Companion to the Pets and Mounts card single source

Leveling pets passively raises your Combat Power through a "Genus Insight" possession effect, not just acquisition and riding.

What it is

The Pets and Mounts card covers how to obtain pets (farming 10 souls per monster) but not what leveling them does for combat power.

How it works

Leveling and collecting pets contributes passive Combat Power through a mechanic described as "Pet Genus Insight" and a "possession effect," where raising a pet's level increases the effect and, in turn, the owner's Combat Power Vortex Gaming, date unknown Vortex Gaming, date unknown.

  • Some individual pets carry unique active-use combat abilities, for example a "Fire Spirit Pup" with a fire attack skill effective against mid-level mobs.
Both sources trace to the same site, so treat this as effectively single-source despite being split across two articles. The Genus Insight/possession-effect terminology is not corroborated elsewhere.

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: this reinforces the Pets and Mounts card's advice to not ignore world mobs while leveling, pet collection has a passive Combat Power payoff on top of mount-riding convenience.

Watch out

Avoid: treating pets as purely cosmetic collectibles and skipping soul farming once you have a mount you like.

Odella and the Odyle Energy Naming Question

Endgame-oriented gathering material single source contested naming kr-era

Odella is likely the same resource this guide already calls Odyle Energy, but no source confirms the two names are identical.

What it is

Odella is a high-tier gathering material, obtained from gathering nodes and defeating an "Inder Boss" encountered while gathering, at roughly 6 Odella per gathering session Vortex Gaming, date unknown.

How it works

The same source states Odella is used to craft "Odella Energy," a Daevanion Crystal-equivalent material, higher-tier wings, and other equipment, with a related crafting process resetting weekly (Wednesday 5 AM per the source), capping players at 7 crafts per week.

Naming flag: the Transcendence/Odyle Energy card and Currencies table both use "Odyle Energy" (also seen as "Grace of Odyle") for the resource spent to open dungeon reward cubes, sourced from a dated AION2Hub patch-notes translation. "Odella Energy," independently described as crafted from the Odella gathering material, is very likely the same underlying KR-language resource under a different fan transliteration, since both are consistently tied to dungeon/endgame progression, but no source explicitly states the two names refer to the same currency. contested, unconfirmed whether Odella Energy = Odyle Energy

Beginner priority

Beginner priority: if you see "Odella" in a KR-era guide and "Odyle Energy" elsewhere in this compendium, don't assume they're unrelated systems, they're most likely translation variants of the same mechanic, but don't assume they're definitely identical either until an official global glossary exists.
